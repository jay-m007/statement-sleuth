Statement Sleuth privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026. Statement Sleuth is a student project by Jay Maurya.

The short version

Statement Sleuth does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything happens on your device.

What the app reads

  • The bank statement file you choose. It is read in memory on your device to find fees and repeating payments. It is not uploaded anywhere and it is not saved.
  • Your reminder list (subscription names, amounts and dates), only if you choose to remember it or turn on phone reminders. It is stored on your device only, and "Clear everything" deletes it and cancels the scheduled reminders.
  • Your name, bank and account text for refund letters, only if you type it in. It stays on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server.
  • It has no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not read your SMS, contacts, location or photos.
  • It does not connect to your bank.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show renewal reminders on the days you pick.
  • Run after restart: so scheduled reminders survive a phone restart.

Limits you should know about

Refund suggestions are rules of thumb and not a promise that a bank will refund anything. This is not legal or financial advice. Anyone who has access to your unlocked phone can open the app and see the reminder list.

How do I get my statement from my bank?

Most banks offer it in net banking or the app: open Account statement, choose a date range (6 to 12 months works best) and download it. Any of these work: PDF, Excel (.xlsx), Word (.docx), CSV, OpenDocument (.ods), OFX/QFX, QIF, TSV, TXT or HTML. Password-protected PDFs are fine, you will be asked for the password.

Not supported: scanned PDFs and photos (they have no text to read) and the old Excel .xls format (open it and Save as .xlsx first). Tip: if your bank gives both, CSV and Excel are the most exact; PDFs are read by position, so check the numbers.

The file needs a date, a description (also called narration or particulars), and either one amount column or separate debit and credit columns.

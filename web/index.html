How do I get a CSV from my bank?

Most banks offer it in net banking or the app: open Account statement, choose a date range (6 to 12 months works best), and pick CSV or Excel as the format. If you only get an Excel file, open it and use Save as CSV. PDFs are not supported yet.

The file needs a date, a description (also called narration or particulars), and either one amount column or separate debit and credit columns.
